# Assignment 4: MAXSER – Lasso and Ridge Regressions

## Objective
Analysis of portfolio optimization methods, computing the global minimum-variance frontier, and implementing the MAXSER rule with Lasso and Ridge regressions

## Dataset
- Ao, Li, and Zheng (RFS 2019) paper dataset
- Mean and covariance matrix of the returns of 103 assets

## Methods used
- Matplotlib for visualization
- Scikit-learn for Lasso and Ridge algorithms
- scipy and statsmodels.api libraries

## Results
- Replication of the key findings in the Ao, Li, and Zheng paper
- Visualization of the importance of statistical regularization in portfolio optimization
